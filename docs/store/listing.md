## Full description

TableTime Staff keeps restaurant schedules, punches, breaks, requests, and team details in one secure workspace.

Managers and owners can:

- Add and update employee records and work locations
- Assign draft shifts and publish weekly schedules
- See who is working and review recorded hours
- Review employee time-off requests
- Keep restaurant data separated by organization and role

Employees can:

- View only their own published schedule
- Clock in and out with server-recorded timestamps
- Start and end breaks
- Review personal punch history
- Submit time-off requests and follow their status

TableTime Staff is designed for busy hospitality teams that need a clear, fast workflow from opening prep through closing time.

An invitation from a restaurant manager is required for employee access. Internet access is required for live scheduling and time-clock updates.
