# TableTime Staff Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 22, 2026

TableTime Staff ("TableTime," "we," "us," or "our") provides restaurant employee scheduling, time-clock, break, request, and workforce-management tools. This policy explains how information is handled when restaurants, managers, and employees use the service.

## Information we process

Depending on how a restaurant configures its workspace, TableTime may process:

- Account information, including name, work email address, authentication identifiers, and encrypted authentication credentials handled by our authentication provider
- Employment information, including job title, work phone, work location, employment status, hourly pay rate, and restaurant role
- Scheduling information, including assigned shifts, shift status, location, position, and notes
- Attendance information, including clock-in and clock-out timestamps, break timestamps, punch source, and related audit records
- Request information, including time-off dates, notes, status, and manager review details
- Restaurant information, including business name, locations, address, timezone, and membership records
- Technical information needed to operate and secure the service, such as session information, error details, and security audit events

TableTime does not currently request device location permission, collect advertising identifiers, sell personal information, or use personal information for targeted advertising.

## How information is used

We use information to authenticate users; operate restaurant schedules and time clocks; display workforce information to authorized users; process employee requests; protect restaurant workspaces; troubleshoot failures; and comply with applicable legal obligations.

## Sharing and service providers

Information is shared only as needed to provide the service or comply with law. TableTime uses Supabase for authentication, database, server functions, and realtime synchronization, and Vercel for web hosting and delivery. Restaurant owners and managers can access workforce information within their own organization according to their role. We do not sell personal information.

## Retention

Restaurants may need to retain scheduling, pay, and attendance records for employment, tax, or legal reasons. We retain information only as long as needed for the service, the restaurant's legitimate recordkeeping obligations, dispute resolution, security, or applicable law. When an account is deleted, direct account access is removed and personal information is deleted or de-identified unless retention is legally required.

## Security

TableTime uses encrypted network connections, role-based access controls, row-level database security, tenant separation, authenticated server functions, and audit records. No security method can guarantee absolute protection.

## Your choices and rights

Users may update eligible profile information through an authorized manager. Users may request access, correction, export, or deletion of personal information. Account-deletion requests are handled subject to restaurant recordkeeping duties and applicable law.

## Children's privacy

TableTime is a workplace service and is not directed to children under 13. Restaurants are responsible for ensuring their use complies with employment and privacy laws applicable to minor employees.

## International processing

Information may be processed in the United States and other locations where our service providers operate. Appropriate safeguards are used where required by law.

## Changes

We may update this policy as the service changes. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
