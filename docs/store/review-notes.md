# App review notes

TableTime Staff is a business workforce app for restaurant teams. Employee accounts are invitation-only. Restaurant owners can create a workspace from the sign-in screen.

## Reviewer test account

Create a dedicated, non-production reviewer restaurant and credentials immediately before submission. Do not place passwords in Git. Add the credentials directly in App Store Connect and Google Play Console review instructions.

Reviewer steps:

1. Sign in with the supplied employee account to view a published personal schedule.
2. Open Time clock to test clock-in, break start/end, and clock-out.
3. Open Requests to submit a short time-off request.
4. Sign in with the supplied manager account to view the team, assign a draft shift, publish the schedule, and review the request.

The app requires an internet connection. If the free Supabase project is retained for review, wake and verify it immediately before submission; an always-on plan is strongly preferred.
